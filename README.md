# Æol — a tidally-locked world

An interactive, living 3D world. One face of the planet burns forever in its star's light; the other freezes in eternal night; between them runs a thin band of permanent twilight — **the Meridian** — where all civilization lives.

## Walk it
Open **[index.html](index.html)** to walk the streets of **Vantage** in first person (click to enter, WASD to walk, mouse to look). The city's people — Clockwrights, Heatfarers, Icebound scholars, and the Unwound — move and work around you in real time, with real-time shadows and a live event ticker.

## See it from orbit
**[aeol_3d.html](aeol_3d.html)** is the planet-scale view: orbit the tidally-locked world, its sun, the glowing terminator band, and click the cities.

## The world
- **The Sunward** — scorched day side
- **The Frostward** — frozen night side
- **The Meridian** — habitable twilight band
- **The Clockwrights' Guild** — owners of time itself
- **The Heatfarers** — suncrystal caravans
- **The Icebound Order** — scholars of the frozen Pale
- **The Unwound** — *"time belongs to no one"*

*The simulation advances one Cycle per day.*
